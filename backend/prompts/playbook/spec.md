---
id: playbook_spec
desc: Guide how to create and use spec.
---

1. **Establish the Intent**: Determine the slide’s narrative role and main takeaway from the request and relevant resources.
2. **Develop the Content**: Select and synthesize supporting material into content elements arranged in reading order.
3. **Develop the Composition**: Define the slide’s layout, grouping, hierarchy, and spatial relationships.
4. **Review and Save**: Check that the message, elements, and layout support the intended takeaway and satisfy applicable requirements, then save with `edit_spec`.
