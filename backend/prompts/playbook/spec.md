---
id: playbook.spec
description: Guide how to create and use spec.
---
1. **Establish the Intent**: Determine the slide's narrative role and main takeaway from the request and relevant resources. Keep the spec focused on slide-specific decisions.
2. **Develop the Content**: Select and synthesize material that supports the core concept. Organize it into meaningful and explicit elements in reading order.
3. **Plan the Composition**: Define the layout, grouping, hierarchy, and spatial relationships that support understanding.
4. **Review and Save**: Check that the message, elements, and composition work together and satisfy applicable requirements. Save the new or revised spec through `edit_spec`.
