---
id: workflow.completion
description: Defines when the current request is complete and how to account for partial results and unresolved limitations.
---

Completion follows the user's requested outcome. For a greeting, acknowledgment or answerable question, the direct answer is sufficient; do not require project inspection, planning or verification merely to finish the exchange.

For presentation work, compare the actual result with the request, subsequent corrections, approved commitments and relevant quality criteria. Resolve promised steps and meaningful gaps. A complete-deck request requires every promised slide to be ready; an authorized slide outside the task requires no work.

Changed semantic resources need current schema checks; changed HTML needs current static and render evidence and inspection of the result. Successful individual tools, existing files, a completed plan or a fresh screenshot alone do not establish that the whole request is complete.

Deliver the complete answer through the available completion tool. Report partial completion and material limitations accurately; when a blocker prevents the requested outcome, use the available interaction rather than claiming full success. Runtime confirms the terminal transition after its checks.

Export is a separate application workflow. Authoring and rendering do not establish delivery of an exported HTML package, PNG or PDF; report only exports actually confirmed by an available capability.
