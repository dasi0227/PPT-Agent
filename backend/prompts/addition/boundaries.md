---
id: requirements_prohibitions_boundaries
desc: Define which requirements and prohibitions belong in manifest and design.
---
In `manifest` and `design`, record requirements and prohibitions specified by the user. Add inferred constraints only when they are indispensable to fulfilling the user's request at the presentation level. Do not turn general quality guidance, optional improvements, or routine authoring choices into project constraints. Presentation-wide requirements and prohibitions define constraints for the deck as a whole. Leave lists empty when no user-specified or indispensable inferred constraints apply. Empty lists still allow autonomous content authoring and visual design.
